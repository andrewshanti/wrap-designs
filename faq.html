Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes do you offer?

Every design comes in three roll sizes, all 30" wide: 30" x 36" (3 ft), 30" x 72" (6 ft) and 30" x 180" (15 ft, our best value). Each size comes in a matte or glossy finish.

How much paper do I need?

As a rough guide, a 3 ft roll wraps 2–3 shirt boxes, a 6 ft roll wraps 5–6, and a 15 ft roll wraps 12–15 medium gifts.

Is there a bundle discount?

Yes. Buy any 3 rolls and save 15%. The discount is applied automatically at checkout, and you can mix and match designs and sizes.

How long does shipping take?

Each roll is printed to order in the USA. Production usually takes 2–5 business days, and US delivery takes another 3–7 business days after that. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

When should I order for Christmas?

For US delivery before December 25, we recommend ordering by December 10. Holiday volumes can add a day or two, so earlier is always safer.

What is the paper like?

The paper is premium-weight and printed edge to edge with vivid, fade-resistant inks. Choose matte for a soft, elegant look or glossy for extra shine. Each roll ships rolled in a protective tube.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within the United States. International shipping is coming soon.

My order arrived damaged or misprinted. What do I do?

We're sorry! Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue, and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Because every roll is made to order just for you, we can't accept returns for change of mind or a wrong size or design. Damaged, defective or misprinted items are always covered (see above).

Can I cancel or change my order?

Orders go into production quickly. Contact us within 2 hours of placing your order and we'll do our best to change or cancel it.

Can I get a custom design or my own dog breed?

We're adding new breeds and designs all season. Send us a message with your request, because the most-requested ones get made first.

How do I contact you?

Use the chat button on any page or email us. We usually reply within one business day.
